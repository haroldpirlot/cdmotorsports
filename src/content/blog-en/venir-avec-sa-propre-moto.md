Some riders really want to be on their own machine, the one they know inside out. I completely understand, and it's absolutely doable. You just need to plan the logistics a little in advance.

To get the bike here, you have two options: road and ferry from Spain if you've got the time and the appetite for the trip, or a specialist shipping company that handles everything. Either way, allow for the lead times.

On the paperwork side, don't cut corners: registration document, green card, a third-party insurance policy valid in Morocco, and above all a repatriation insurance that actually covers off-road riding. That's the kind of detail people forget and then get stuck with at the border.

Once you're here, even on your own bike, you get the full support package: the 4x4 following along, luggage transport, daily mechanical check-ups. You ride light, I handle the logistics. [The 4x4 and companions option](/en/option-4x4).

Riding your own motorcycle means keeping your feel and your reflexes on terrain that has nothing in common with home. My job is to make sure everything runs smoothly so all you have to do is enjoy it.
