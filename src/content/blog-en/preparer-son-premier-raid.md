The question I always get before someone's first trip is: "Is this really for me?" Most of the time, the answer is yes. If you already ride off-road, motocross or enduro, even just on weekends, you've got the foundation to come along. The rest you learn on the trail.

What really matters is the drive and a bit of off-road experience. A motivated beginner fits right in with a small guided group: we move together, at a pace that gives everyone the time to find their rhythm and build confidence. [Discover my raids](/en/#raids).

On the fitness side, you don't need to train like a pro rider. But stacking long days in the saddle is more tiring than people imagine, especially in the heat. A few weeks of cardio before you come, and you'll enjoy the later stages a whole lot more. [My page on physical preparation](/en/actualites/preparation-physique-raid).

For gear, you bring your own protection (helmet, goggles, back protector, elbow and knee pads, boots, gloves) and something to protect you from the sun. The motorcycle, I provide ready to ride: a KOVE 450 Rally dialed in for the terrain. <a href="https://kove-racing.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">KOVE Racing</a>.

The real learning curve is reading the terrain and navigating. And that's my job: I'm here to pass on the right instincts, stage after stage. You just ride, observe, and enjoy yourself.
