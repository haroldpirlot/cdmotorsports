If I chose southern Morocco to take people riding, it isn't by chance. In just a few days, you cover every face of rally-raid: fast flowing pistes you can roll through, rocky plateaus that make you read every meter, dry riverbeds, dune ridges, the passes of the Anti-Atlas. Nowhere else do you find such variety packed so tight. [My raids](/en/#raids).

Then there's the history. These landscapes have seen the great African adventures roll through, and you can still feel that spirit when you ride here. It's hard not to think about it as you crest a trail.

The climate helps a lot: between the Atlantic coast and the mountains, Morocco offers long windows where you can ride in good conditions for most of the year. <a href="https://www.visitmorocco.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tourism in Morocco</a>.

But in the end, what stays with you isn't just the riding. It's the villages clinging to the mountainside, a shared tea when you arrive, the welcome from the people. Here, every stage is a chance encounter. [Discover Morocco](/en/#maroc).
