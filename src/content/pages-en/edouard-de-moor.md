    bigTitle: "A life on <em>two wheels.</em>"
    paragraphs:
      - "I'm 32, and motorcycles have been part of my life since I was five."
      - "I grew up on two wheels and in competition from the very beginning, with many years spent in motocross. At 19 I switched to enduro, a discipline that took my riding to a new level — including the chance to join the Husqvarna Trophy in the Belgian Championship, and later to continue the adventure with Yamaha Belgium."
      - "Those years gradually pushed me toward what has now become my single focus: <em>rally-raid.</em>"
      - "Alongside the raids, I'm also a test rider for new models with the Belgian outlet <a href=\"https://onerace.be/\" target=\"_blank\" rel=\"noopener noreferrer\">OneRace</a>."
  - meta: "The project"
    bigTitle: "Why <em>CDM Motorsport?</em>"
    paragraphs:
      - "CDM Motorsport is the result of a long reflection. The goal was to build my own business while staying inside the world I want to keep living in: motorcycles, the terrain, and above all, competition."
      - "Rally-raid sits right at the intersection of those pieces. It lets me share my experience with my clients, guide them into the discipline, and make them live something strong — while showing them a country I know particularly well. And it keeps a big part of my time free for my own training and my own racing goals."
      - "Morocco is ideal ground, both for training and for racing. The variety of its tracks and landscapes makes it a true playground to discover rally-raid in exceptional conditions."
  - meta: "The mindset"
    bigTitle: "The <em>CDM Motorsport</em> spirit."
    paragraphs:
      - "Through CDM Motorsport, my first goal is to let you live your own adventure: your passion, the sharing, and the honest side of this beautiful country. I'll be happy to welcome you at the start line of the raid you choose, to ride with you from day one to the last, and to pass on a part of what competition and years of riding have taught me."
      - "The idea isn't just to show you a destination on a motorcycle — it's to pass on as much know-how as I can along the way. Reading the terrain, learning to move on the tracks and experiencing rally-raid from the inside, at your own level, is part of the adventure."
      - "Rally-raid is also about sharing and solidarity. The moments we live together, the conversations, the problems we face on the tracks and the satisfaction of solving them, all feed the experience. The goal is to give you the keys to progress, to build confidence in the face of difficulty, and above all to enjoy every moment in an exceptional setting."
      - "Beyond the bike, there are the people. The ones you'll meet, the ones you'll ride with, and the ties that build up as the kilometers roll by are a huge part of what you'll bring home from a raid. Because in the end, it isn't just the kilometers you remember, it's everything you lived and shared along the way."
      - "CDM Motorsport's purpose, really, is to be the best possible companion for your adventure, with one essential priority: seeing you cross the finish line safely, but above all with a smile. Beyond the kilometers and the challenges, my real reward is seeing you leave happy with the experience, already thinking about the next one."
