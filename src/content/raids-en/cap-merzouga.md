From Ouarzazate to the Merzouga dunes: across the Deep South, into the Erg Chebbi, then back through the desert tracks. Six days to reach one of the most legendary ergs in Morocco.

On the KOVE 450 Rally, in a small guided group, with 4×4 support and full logistics.
