Something new is taking shape, and this time we're leaving Morocco for Spain. A six-day raid starting from Alicante, on the Costa Blanca — just a short flight away.

Behind the Costa Blanca beaches hides a beautiful and still underrated playground. As soon as you head inland, you find arid sierras, tracks climbing into the mountains, hilltop villages, and panoramic views dropping to the Mediterranean. A greener, more mineral setting than the Moroccan desert, with its own feel.

It's the ideal format for a European adventure, perfect for off-season riding when Morocco is too hot, or to discover rally-raid without flying far. You get the full CDM Motorsport spirit: small group, full support, and the freedom of the tracks — in a new setting.

The route, dates and price are still being finalized. Drop me a line if you'd like to be the first to hear when bookings open.
