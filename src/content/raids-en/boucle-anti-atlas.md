A loop out of Ouarzazate that drops you into the Anti-Atlas: gorges, mountain passes, high plateaus and Berber villages. Five days to take in a condensed snapshot of Moroccan geography.

On the KOVE 450 Rally, in a small guided group, with 4×4 support and full logistics.
